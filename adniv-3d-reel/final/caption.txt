Peeli light kahan gayi? 💡 India's streets went from yellow to white, and here's why.

Raat ki woh peeli roshni yaad hai? Woh thi sodium lamp, jiski roshni mein laal, hara, neela sab ek jaisa dikhta tha. Phir 5 January 2015 ko Street Lighting National Programme (SLNP) shuru hua, aur sodium lamps ki jagah LED street lights lagne lagi.

Why did India's street lights change from yellow to white?
Because the Street Lighting National Programme, launched on 5 January 2015, replaced old sodium lamps with energy-efficient LED street lights. LED gives whiter, clearer light and uses far less electricity.

The numbers (Source: PIB, Ministry of Power, 6 Jan 2025):
✅ 1.34 crore+ LED street lights installed
✅ 9,001 million units+ electricity saved every year
✅ 6.2 million tonnes (62 lakh tonnes) CO2 reduced every year
