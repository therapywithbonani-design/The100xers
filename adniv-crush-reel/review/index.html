02

Lighting fix · before / after

Per clip: white balance read from neutral surfaces (the light sources themselves are never "corrected"), exposure set in linear light, a gentle shadow lift, and a bounded highlight roll-off. That roll-off takes clipped pixels on the rings and panels from 0.4–0.9% to 0%, so the ring keeps its edge-to-centre gradient instead of going flat white. The 4000K fixtures land clean neutral white; the 3000K domes stay warm. One shared finish goes on top: a light S-curve, +3% saturation, a faint warm-mid / neutral-highlight balance. There's no tint or wash over the footage. 4K → 1080 × 1920 uses Lanczos with light sharpening.
