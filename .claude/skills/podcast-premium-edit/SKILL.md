# Podcast Premium Edit (+ Before/After portfolio format)

Source: 18s editor-portfolio ad. Raw podcast clip ("NO EDIT", B&W card behind) vs the edited version ("FINAL", front card). This playbook covers both: **A** = the edit style inside FINAL, **B** = the showcase ad around it.

---

## A. The edit style (FINAL)

### Footage & grade
- 2 angles minimum: **tight mid-shot** (mic, crossed arms) + **wide studio** (whole chair, pendant lamp, bare grey walls, floor). Cut between them every 2–4s on sentence boundaries.
- Grade: neutral/cool grey world, contrasty, warm skin & a warm accent (mustard trousers/sofa) → orange-gold pops. Slight vignette.
- Punch-in on the hook word (100→120%) + a **face-tracking frame** overlay: red corner brackets around the face + "!!" → looks like a "smart reframe", used in the first second.

### Caption system (the signature)
- **Stacked mixed-size lines**, centred over the body: small connector words (white, 22–28px @720w, Inter/SF Medium) + one **huge keyword** (white, Inter Display Black/Bold 90–130px, tight tracking), e.g. "*And the* **reason** *why I say*", "**9/10** times".
- Accent treatments (one per phrase, max):
  - **Orange script** word under the keyword ("disguise") — brush/handwritten (free: *Caveat Brush / Yellowtail*), #F08A24.
  - **Yellow highlighter** behind a word ("small") — marker swipe drawn left→right 6f, #F5E663, multiply.
  - **Gold title** for topic cards: small serif "the truth about" + big condensed caps **PERFECTIONISM** (Bebas/Anton) in gold gradient #C9A25A.
  - **Red tag**: tiny red rounded box under the line with a white 2-word explainer ("Meaning that").
  - **Condensed caps block**: 2 lines of white heavy condensed caps ("TO AND / DOING THE") for fast sentences.
- Reveal: each word **blur-in** (blur 12→0 + 10px rise, 5–6f); keyword lands with a 105→100% scale. Out: quick blur-out.
- Fractions/stats animate ("9/1" → "9/10") — number morph or slot roll.
- Glow: soft white outer glow on keywords over busy backgrounds.

### Graphic moves
1. **Shape-mask collage** — subject full-body cut-out on clean grey; a **plus/cross-shaped mask** filled with moody orange b-roll photos sits behind him; caption sits on top. (CapCut: mask "rectangle" ×2 or a PNG cross mask; Resolve: Fusion polygon mask.)
2. **Floating cut-outs** — small product/photo cards (books, phone, polaroid of a person) pop in around the subject with drop shadows, slight rotation, in sync with what's mentioned.
3. **Scrapbook wide shot** — subject cut-out on grey + layered paper elements: "About Me" card, checklist note ("Creative Process"), polaroid portrait, sticky notes, handwritten blue scribbles, camera on tripod, plant. Elements pop in one by one (3–4f each) then hold — a "who I am" credibility moment.
4. **Lamp flicker / light-on** in the wide shot as a transition beat.
5. Watermark: tiny white wordmark bottom-centre on every frame (brand/channel).

### Pacing
- New caption every 0.4–0.8s; angle change every 2–4s; one graphic set-piece every 4–6s.
- Hook (0–1s): face-tracking bracket + "!!" + stat word.

## B. Showcase / ad format: "NO EDIT vs FINAL" (sell editing / content services)
- Background: soft grey studio gradient (#E9E9E9 → #BDBDBD) with spotlight from top-left.
- **Back card** (left, rotated −8°, 60% size, B&W/desaturated): raw clip with tiny black label **NO EDIT**.
- **Front card** (centre-right, 0°, rounded 18px, big shadow): edited clip, label **FINAL** (white on black tag, top-left).
- Below cards: small caps "YOUR CLIPS CAN LOOK LIKE THIS TOO" + huge condensed **DM ME TO START** (Bebas Neue/Anton, #1A1A1A). Intro: text blur-in; cards slide in from left with rotation settle.
- Both cards play **synced** so viewers compare the same moment.
- Variants for clients: "BEFORE / AFTER" (renovation, salon, fitness), "RAW / GRADED", "PHONE / PRO".

### Conversion
- Single CTA: DM keyword ("DM 'EDIT'") → auto-reply with portfolio + pricing + booking link.
- KPIs: DMs per 1k reach, DM→call booked %, cost per qualified lead, close rate.

## Adapting by niche
- Coaches/founders/real estate agents: same caption system, swap scrapbook to credentials (listings sold, certificates, client logos).
- Clothing/jewellery founders: gold title + floating product cut-outs; scrapbook with fabric swatches/sketches.
- Café/food: wide shot of kitchen; floating cut-outs of dishes with prices.
