# Editorial Lookbook — Reel Editing Playbook

Reverse-engineered from a 15s, 9:16 (720x1280, 30fps) premium Indian ethnicwear reel
(single outfit, single model, studio set). **No voice, no captions, no on-screen copy except the logo.**
Feel: quiet luxury, magazine spread → moving campaign. The product is the only story.

---

## 1. Structure (15s template)

| Beat | Time | What happens | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cover** | 0–0.3s | Full-frame **B&W still** (seated, profile, detail-rich pose) | Static, desaturated, soft contrast |
| **Photo-card build** | 0.3–1.9s | Colour catalogue photos pop onto the B&W still one at a time → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 in a **2×2 grid** | Hard pop-in (no fade, no border, no shadow), ~0.35–0.5s apart, on the beat |
| **Flash burst** | 1.9–2.3s | Snap to full-frame colour video; 3–4 shots at **3–4 frames each** (hands close-up → back twirl → front dupatta spread) | Hard cuts, the "drop" of the music |
| **Look rhythm** | 2.3–4.7s | Full-length looks: front, dupatta spread, front stand | Cuts every ~0.4s, beat-synced |
| **Detail section** | 4.7–7.4s | Mid → hands w/ fabric (alta/mehendi, rings) → face + earrings → side profile → **neckline embroidery macro** | Cuts every ~1–1.2s, slow drift/push |
| **Movement** | 7.4–10.7s | Model walks toward camera / twirls, fabric flowing | One long take, slowed (50–60%) |
| **Hero + wordmark** | 10.7–13.5s | Front-facing hero hold, slight smile | Slow push-in; logo fades in at ~12s, centred on chest |
| **Out** | 13.5–14.8s | Fade to black (1s) | Logo stays until black |

Pacing curve: **build → explode → slow down → hold.** Shot lengths go 0.1s → 0.4s → 1s → 3s.

---

## 2. Cuts & transitions

- **Only hard cuts + one fade to black** at the end. No zooms, whooshes, glitches, swipes.
- **Photo-card pop:** cards appear instantly at 100% (optional 2–3 frame scale 105%→100%). No drop shadow, no frame — the cream background of the photo itself is the border. Grid: 2 columns, small gutter (~2% width), centred, covering the middle 60% of the frame, faces of the cover still left visible at top.
- **B&W → colour** is the hook: the first full-colour video frame is the payoff.
- **Flash burst:** 3–4 clips of 3–4 frames each right on the music drop.
- **Every cut on the beat** of the track; detail shots on bar changes.

## 3. Camera & shot list (give to the shoot team)

Locked-off or slow gimbal push, 4K, 50/60fps for slow-mo.
1. Seated profile still (for B&W cover) — jewellery + hairstyle visible
2. Catalogue stills ×4 on the same set: front, side seated/twirl, back, smile close
3. Hands crossed over fabric (mehendi/alta, rings) — close
4. Back to camera, twirl (fabric flare)
5. Front, dupatta/stole spread with arms out
6. Front stand, hands clasped (full length)
7. Waist-up, face turning to profile (earrings)
8. Side-on 3/4 walk-past (sleeve detail, embroidery)
9. **Macro of neckline / embellishment** — slow rack focus
10. Walk toward camera, slow motion (fabric movement)
11. Hero: front, mid-shot, soft smile, 4–5s hold for logo

## 4. Look & grade

- **Tonal set**: backdrop, props and floor in one soft colour that harmonises with the garment (here cream/butter with a mustard outfit; a vintage gramophone + arch alcove as the only props).
- Grade: warm, lifted blacks, low contrast, soft highlight roll-off, skin natural; no heavy LUT, no vignette.
- B&W cover: neutral grey, gentle contrast, no crushed blacks.
- One **pop colour accent** in styling (red alta on hands, emerald earrings) to draw the eye in detail shots.

## 5. Typography

- **Only the brand wordmark.** Lowercase or title-case thin/light serif (free matches: **Cormorant Garamond Light, Playfair Display Regular, Italiana**), white at ~90% opacity, small (~25% frame width), centred mid-frame (on the chest of the hero).
- Animation: opacity 0→100% over ~0.5s; no movement.
- Optional (for conversion, keep it equally quiet): a second line in tiny tracked-out caps under the logo, e.g. `THE ZARI EDIT · SHOP NOW`, Montserrat Light, letter-spacing 300.

## 6. Audio

- Music-only. Soft, elegant track with a clear **drop at ~2s** (instrumental, sitar/strings/lo-fi house for ethnicwear; minimal piano/house for western).
- Optional subtle SFX: camera shutter "click" on each photo-card pop.

---

## 7. Adapting by niche

| Niche | Changes |
|---|---|
| **Ethnicwear / bridal** | As above; detail section = embroidery, zari, borders, jewellery |
| **Western / streetwear** | Cover still in B&W urban location; flash burst faster (2 frames); grade cooler; walk shot outdoors |
| **Jewellery** | Photo cards = piece flat-lays; detail section = macro sparkle, ear/neck/hand try-on; slower overall (1.5s detail cuts) |
| **Multi-outfit collection** | One look per beat in the rhythm section; photo-card grid = 4 different outfits (the "collection" reveal) |
| **Beauty / accessories** | Cover still = product B&W; cards = shades/variants |

## 8. Conversion layer (always add)

This style is brand-building by default — make it sell:
- **Hook (0–2s)** = grid build: shows 4 looks instantly → raises "where can I buy this" intent.
- End frame: wordmark + tiny CTA line ("New drop · Link in bio" / "DM 'ZARI' for price").
- Caption: price + sizes + fabric in the first line; pin comment with the product link.
- Use the hero frame as the cover image.
- Run as a Reels ad with "Shop now" button; also cut a 6s version (cover → grid → flash burst → hero + logo).
- KPIs: 3s hold rate >55%, outbound link CTR >1%, cost per add-to-cart, ROAS (target 3×+); organic: saves & shares per 1k reach, DMs with the keyword.

## 9. Editor checklist
1. Pick the most editorial still → B&W → full-frame cover.
2. Pop in 4 catalogue photos one by one into a 2×2 grid, on the beat.
3. Cut to colour on the drop with a 3–4 shot flash burst.
4. Look shots at ~0.4s, then detail shots at ~1s, then one slow-mo movement take.
5. Hero hold, wordmark fades in, + quiet CTA line.
6. Fade to black in 1s. Total 12–18s.
7. No captions, no stickers, no emojis.
