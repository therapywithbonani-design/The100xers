# Luxe Editorial Motion (+ 2×2 showcase format)

Source: 5.6s showcase: 4 edits playing at once in a 2×2 grid (AI-generated model footage), badge "… cooked 🌸 · Comment KEYWORD for the full guide". Four sub-styles, all in one luxury system.

## Shared look
- Sets: dark plum/burgundy textured walls, warm beige plaster wall with window light, navy studio; candle/brass lamps, wood floors, leather chair.
- Wardrobe: satin/silk in blush, chocolate, ivory — tonal with the set.
- Grade: rich, warm highlights, deep plum/navy shadows, glowing skin, soft bloom.
- Palette: plum #3A1530, navy #0E1A2E, blush #C99AA6, cream #F1E6D6, chocolate #4A2A1E, gold #C9A25A.
- Fonts: **modern sans** (Inter/General Sans) for big single words; **high-contrast serif** (Playfair Display / Bodoni Moda / *Fraunces*) roman + italic; **elegant script** (*Allura / Great Vibes*) for one emotional word.

## Sub-style 1 — Glass card + stat
- A **frosted-glass rounded phone-shaped card** (blur 30, white/plum 12% fill, 1px light stroke, Dynamic-Island pill on top) sits **behind the subject** (person cut-out).
- Inside the card: small sans line + big serif word ("Sales keep **Soaring**").
- **Stat chip** floats at the card edge: outlined pill "+24%".
- Transition out: whole frame **defocus blur** → next shot.

## Sub-style 2 — One giant word
- Navy glass card behind the subject; **one huge white sans word at a time** ("Still / losing / to / edits"), centred in the card top, swaps every 0.3–0.4s on the beat (hard switch + 2f blur).
- Small twinkling bokeh dots around the card; tiny gold line marker above.

## Sub-style 3 — Magazine serif beside the model
- Model full-length against a plain wall; big serif type in the empty wall space:
  "*never*" (italic, small) → "**average.**" (roman bold, big); "*choose* **premium.**"; small sans "Can people" + giant serif "**feel**".
- Thin hairline frame/rectangle draws on around the type; a thin swash line draws in.
- Reveal: letters rise + fade with stagger (2f/letter), italic word first, roman word lands.
- Colour: chocolate #3A2418 on beige; ivory on plum.

## Sub-style 4 — Script + polaroids
- On maroon wall: white serif line types in ("If you edit every **frame**", "all by **hand**").
- Script word ("*Never*") writes on with a glow; sub-line "expect to **scale**" in red serif.
- **Polaroid/photo cards fly in** from the top-right corner, fanned and rotated, with glass-shard sparkles.

## Motion-graphic interludes (between talking shots, 0.6–1.2s each)
- **Stat box countdown**: rounded outlined square, number counting 24% → 11% → 3% → 0% with small label below ("turnaround time").
- **Chrome/rose-gold glossy orb** centred on plum bokeh; **floating UI chips** orbit it ("Every client adds extra *work*": "Revision round 3", "Late-night edits", "New onboarding", "Extra calls").
- **Particle burst**: dots on navy converge into a logo/icon tile ("ideas" → logo card → "Ask [Brand]" on cream).
- Use these to visualise the *problem* (orb + chips = workload) and *solution* (burst → logo).

## 2×2 showcase format
- 4 edits in a 2×2 grid, no gutters, all synced; centred white pill badge across the middle: **"[Tool/Brand] cooked 🌸"** + "Comment **KEYWORD** for the full guide" (keyword in coral).
- Length 5–8s → high loop/replay rate.
- Use for: agency style menu ("4 ways we can edit your reel"), product in 4 colourways, 4 looks from one shoot.

## Conversion
- Comment-keyword lead magnet ("Comment LUXE") → auto-DM with guide/portfolio → booking.
- Brand version: replace stat chips with real results (+24% sales, 0% wasted time); end on "Ask [Brand]" card = logo + offer.
- KPIs: replay rate, keyword comments per 1k reach, DM→lead %, CPL; for brand ads: CTR, ROAS.

## Adapting by niche
- Jewellery/fashion: sub-style 3 (serif beside model) + polaroids of the collection.
- Beauty/salon: glass card with before/after stat chips.
- B2B/agency/SaaS: orb + workload chips → logo burst; one-giant-word captions.
- Real estate luxury: magazine serif on interior walls ("*live* **above.**").
