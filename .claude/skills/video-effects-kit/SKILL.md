## Showcase format: "17 Video Effects" list reel
- **Background:** white with faint grey grid-paper lines (#F5F6F8 + #E6E8EC 1px lines every 24px).
- **Title (top):** "**17** Video Effects" — number in blue #2F6BFF, rest black, geometric sans bold (Manrope/Inter Bold); line 2: *Brand* in italic serif (Instrument Serif / Playfair Italic) + "You Can Create" bold sans.
- **List (under title):** two columns of 9 + 8 numbered items, mono numbers (JetBrains Mono) + sans names, grey #9AA0A6; **active row = blue-tinted pill with blue outline**, black bold text; done items get a ✓.
- **Card:** rounded 18px white-bordered card (≈60% width), slight drop shadow, counter chip "01/17" top-left (black pill, white mono), effect name in a **blue pill** bottom-centre, small round avatar sticker bottom-right outside the card.
- **Switch:** card content hard-cuts to next effect on the beat; list highlight jumps to the next row in the same frame.
- **Outro (CTA, ~5s):** list collapses → card becomes a **checklist of all 17 with green ticks** + avatar above; IG comment bar types **LIST** → toast "✓ "LIST" sent" → DM preview "sent you the full list" → follow card with **Follow** button; caption line word-by-word: "Comment "LIST" and I'll send the full *list* in your *DMs*. Don't forget to *follow*" (italic serif on key words, blur-in).
- Ends by looping back to effect 01 (seamless replay).
