# Pinteresty Style — Reel Editing Playbook

Reverse-engineered from a 97s, 9:16 (720x1280, 30fps) Indian creator haul reel
("I was scammed" → Banjara Market décor haul). Approx. 60 cuts in 97s = **avg shot ~1.6s**.
Feel: soft, girly, diary-like, "Pinterest board come alive" — but paced like a TikTok.

---

## 1. The structure (story spine)

| Beat | Time | What happens | Edit treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–1.3s | Shock line to camera ("I WAS SCAMMED") | Full **B&W**, huge red stacked text, word-by-word pop |
| **Context** | 1.3–6s | "Look, if you can't bargain… no point going to X" | Cut to colour; b-roll of location with caption over it |
| **Promise** | 6–11s | "So let me show you what I got" | Short b-roll burst (night/market, handheld, motion blur) |
| **Branded reveal** | ~11s | "from BANJARA MARKET" | Script lead-in word + big serif keyword typed on |
| **Item loop ×N** | 12–80s | Show item → name it → price → reaction | Price tracker updates, keyword title, doodle callout |
| **Story aside** | mid | Brother teaching bargaining / "and I was like…" | Night b-roll + **B&W 4-panel photo-strip** reaction |
| **Favourite pick** | ~65s | Build-up "my FAVOURITE pick" | Hands-clap, B&W "but…" aside, big serif "BEAUTY" |
| **Wrap** | last 8s | "and that was my BANJARA HAUL" + witty sign-off ("no comments.") | Script + yellow serif title, end on deadpan line |

Rule: every 3–6 seconds something **new appears** (item, price, sticker, b-roll, B&W shift).

---

## 2. Cuts & pacing

- **Jump cuts** on every breath/filler — no pauses left in. Speaker stays roughly same framing; alternate **punch-ins (100% → ~115–130%)** between sentences to hide jumps.
- **Item hold shots:** product pushed toward lens (fills 50–70% of frame, face partly hidden). Hold 1.5–3s.
- **B-roll cutaways:** 1–2.5s each, handheld, natural motion blur, warm daylight or neon night. Captions stay ON over b-roll.
- **Re-shoots in different outfits/rooms are fine** — the style tolerates continuity jumps (casual-diary feel).
- Cut ON the action (lifting item, turning bowl, hand gesture).
- Avg shot length target: **1.2–2s** for talking; never > 4s without a new overlay.

## 3. Transitions (all hard cuts — no swipes/whooshes)

1. **Hard cut** — default (~90% of cuts).
2. **Colour → B&W switch** — used for hook, sarcastic asides, flashbacks, "problem" moments ("I didn't get a proper look… SCAMMED", "don't know how I'll take this back"). Return to colour = return to the happy/product moment.
3. **B&W photo-strip montage** — black background, 1 rounded-corner vertical frame (~60% width, centred) of the speaker, 3–4 quick B&W reaction stills/clips at ~0.25–0.4s each (salute, smirk, wink). Used after "and I was like…".
4. **Snap punch-in** on the punchline word.
5. **Picture-in-picture sticker** — tiny square photo of the person being talked about (bottom-right, white border) for 1s.

## 4. Typography system (the signature)

Five text layers. Free equivalents in brackets.

| Layer | Look | Font (free match) | Colour (approx.) | Position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Running captions** | Small, bold sans, sentence case, 1–2 lines, soft shadow | Instagram "Classic" / Helvetica Bold (**Inter Bold / Arial Bold**) ~34px @1080w | White #FFFFFF, 30% black shadow | Centre-lower third (~60–70% height), centred |
| **B. Hook text** | Stacked, ALL CAPS, condensed heavy, word per line, dark drop shadow | **Anton / Bebas Neue / Impact** | Red #E3161B | Centre-lower, overlapping chest |
| **C. Keyword title** | Script lead-in word + big serif keyword | Script: **Sacramento / Great Vibes / Allura**. Serif: **Abril Fatface / DM Serif Display / Playfair Display Black** | Serif in soft brand tone: peach #F2A27A, pastel blue #BFD9FF, cream-yellow #FFE27A, pink #F27BB3 | Over the product / mid-frame |
| **D. Cutesy accents** | Bubbly/rounded with white outline, or rounded sans | **Chewy / Fredoka / Baloo** | Pink #F05BA0, sage #8DB33A for "SAGE green" | Next to product |
| **E. Info tracker** | Typewriter/mono, small, persistent | **Space Mono / Courier Prime / VT323** | Mint #CFF5C8 with small red "SALE" tag icon | Top-left, safe zone (~6% from top/left) |

**Keyword title animation (most important move):**
1. Script lead-in types on word-by-word, synced to speech ("this is" → "this is for your").
2. On the keyword, the serif word **pops in big** (scale 120%→100%, 4–6 frames) — "BRUSHES".
3. Add a matching **emoji/mini sticker** right after (🧴 🪥 ☕ 🎀).
4. Optional small script descriptor underneath ("dispenser", "holder", "set") — "HANDWASH / dispenser".
Hold 1–1.5s, then hard-cut off.

**Info tracker:** stays on screen for whole product section; text **types on** character by character when value changes ("Price:" → "Price:150rs" → "Price:300rs (100x3)"). Blank "Price:" when talking between items. Swap "Price" for any key fact: `Calories:`, `Rating:`, `Wait time:`, `Cost/serve:`.

**Caption rules:** captions only for non-keyword lines; when a keyword title appears, the running caption disappears. Occasionally upgrade a caption to ALL CAPS for emphasis ("I LOVE IT…", "FAVOURITE PICK"), or red for a joke ("SCAMMED 😂😂😂").

## 5. Graphic stickers & doodles

- **Dashed white circle** around a product detail + label ("there's a PINK BOW").
- **Curved hand-drawn arrow** (pink/white) pointing to person or product + label in caps ("MERE BHAIYA", "THIS BOWL", "VERY CUTE" with two arrows).
- **#1 #2 #3** numbering, yellow serif, top-right when showing variants.
- Emoji used inline with titles, never as background spam.
- Everything pastel; one strong accent (red) reserved for drama.

## 6. Colour grade & look

- Natural soft daylight, slightly warm, lifted shadows, low contrast, gentle skin glow (Pinterest "clean girl" look). No heavy LUT.
- Neutral home background (white walls, beige bedsheet); wardrobe in one muted pastel (sage) that ties to text palette.
- B&W segments: full desaturate, slightly raised contrast.
- Night b-roll left vibrant (neon/fairy lights) for contrast with the soft day talking shots.

## 7. Audio
- Voice-led, clean phone/lav audio. Optional low trending/lo-fi bed under b-roll only (verify per brand).
- Small pop/"ding" SFX on keyword title pops and price changes; camera shutter on photo-strip frames.

## 8. How to adapt

### Talking reel + b-roll (coach, founder, service business)
- Hook in B&W with red stacked text (pain point: "I WAS LOSING CLIENTS").
- Tracker top-left = proof metric (`Leads:` `Revenue:` `Step 1/5`).
- Each point = script lead-in + serif keyword ("the secret is … CONSISTENCY").
- B&W for the "before/problem", colour for the "after/solution".

### Cute cafe reel
- Hook: B&W shot of sad desk coffee → "I FOUND THE CUTEST CAFÉ" red text → snap to colour.
- Item loop = each dish/drink pushed to lens; tracker `Price:` (and `Rating: 9/10`).
- Keyword titles: "this is their … STRAWBERRY MATCHA 🍓". Dashed circle on latte art; arrow "THE VIEW".
- B-roll: door/signage, barista pour, interiors, 1–2s each.
- Photo-strip: friend's reactions after first sip.
- Close: "and that was … CAFE NAME" serif title + location sticker.

### Product / haul / D2C brand
- Exactly the original structure; tracker shows price + offer ("Price: ₹499 (was ₹899)").

## 9. Conversion layer (always add)
- End card / last line CTA in the same type system: script "save this for" + serif "YOUR NEXT VISIT" / "DM 'BOOK'" / "LINK IN BIO".
- Put the location/offer in the tracker so viewers screenshot it.
- Pin comment with price list/menu.
- Suggested KPIs: 3s hold rate >60% (hook), saves & shares per reach, profile visits → DMs/bookings (CPL), link CTR for products.

## 10. Execution checklist for editors
1. Cut silences; punch-in alternate takes.
2. Hook in B&W + red stacked text, first 1.5s.
3. Running captions (Layer A) throughout.
4. Keyword titles (Layer C) on every item/key point, synced word-by-word.
5. Persistent tracker top-left with type-on updates.
6. 1–2 doodle callouts per item.
7. At least 1 B&W aside + 1 photo-strip reaction per 60s.
8. B-roll every 10–15s.
9. Grade soft & warm; keep text in 9:16 safe zone (avoid bottom 20% / right UI rail).
10. End on witty one-liner + CTA.
