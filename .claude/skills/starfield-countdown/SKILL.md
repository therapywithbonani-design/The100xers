# Starfield Countdown — Numbered Showcase Playbook

Reverse-engineered from a 16s, 9:16 (720x1280, 30fps) faceless listicle:
"10 [Tool] Animation styles (To Go Viral)" — the SAME scene (man walking a dog) shown in 10 visual styles,
then a CTA grid: "Comment 'Animation' for the Prompts".
The original has **no talking head**; section 6 covers the talking-head version.

---

## 1. Layout (fixed for the whole list section)

| Zone | Position @720x1280 | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | full frame | Pure black #050505 with a sparse white **starfield** (1–2px dots, 20–60% opacity, slow drift) + soft grey radial glow behind the card |
| **Title** | top, y≈40–140, centred | Line 1: **"10"** white bold sans + **brand/keyword in italic serif, coral-red #E5533D** + small asterisk/logo icon. Line 2: **"Animation"** bold-italic sans + "styles" regular sans. Line 3: "(To Go Viral)" small grey |
| **Numbered list** | left 40%, x≈20, y≈190–420, 10 rows ~26px apart | "1)" … "10)" — names revealed only as each item plays |
| **Showcase card** | right ~45% width (x≈150–310 scaled ×2.25 → ≈340–700 @720), y≈165–445 scaled | Rounded rect (12–16px), 9:16-ish, subtle drop shadow, content fills card |

Fonts: **Inter/SF Pro Bold & Bold Italic** for sans, **Playfair Display Italic / Instrument Serif Italic** for the coral keyword.

## 2. The list mechanic (the retention hook)
- Start: only numbers visible "1) … 10)" — curiosity about the 9 hidden names.
- Active item: **number in coral-red**, name in **white bold**.
- Past items: number + name drop to **grey ~45% opacity**, regular weight.
- Future items: number only, white 70%.
- Name appears the instant the card changes (2–3 frame fade).

## 3. Timing & transitions
- Intro: title fades/blurs in over first ~0.3s while item 1 is already playing (no empty intro).
- **~1.2–1.4s per item** (10 items ≈ 12.7s). Fast enough that viewers rewatch / pause to read → watch-time + replays.
- **Transition = horizontal wipe inside the card**: next style's card slides in from the right edge with a thin light edge/shadow, 5–8 frames, ease-in-out. Card frame never moves — only its content changes.
- Each card is a **short looping animation** (subject keeps walking) so motion never stops.
- Same subject/composition across all items → the *only* change is the style, which makes the comparison instantly readable.

## 4. Outro / CTA (last ~3s)
- Hard cut to **6-card grid** on the starfield: top row of 3 drifts slowly **left**, bottom row of 3 drifts **right** (infinite marquee).
- Centred between rows: **Comment "*Keyword*" for the Prompts** — white bold sans, keyword in coral italic serif with quotes.
- Fade-in 0.3s; hold to end; reel loops cleanly back to item 1.

## 5. Audio
- Music-only, upbeat/trending; ideally a beat per item so each wipe lands on a beat (≈1.3s ≈ 92 BPM per 2 beats). Optional soft whoosh per wipe.
- (Audio not verified from the source — mixed at about -19 dB mean; treat music choice as a recommendation.)

## 6. Talking-head version (3 options)
1. **Speaker-in-card:** card = speaker clip in each item (e.g. 10 outfits, 10 hairstyles, 10 locations). List left still reveals names. Best for fashion, beauty, travel.
2. **Voice-over countdown:** speaker voices each item over the faceless layout; add one-line caption under the card (white bold 22px). Open with a 1.5s full-screen speaker hook ("Here are 10 … that go viral"), then cut to the layout.
3. **Speaker bottom, list top:** speaker in lower 45% (like dark-split-explainer); top half = title + list + small card. Use for tools/tips listicles.

## 7. Adapting by niche
| Niche | Showcase card content | CTA keyword |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing | Same model, 10 outfits / 10 ways to style one piece | "Comment LOOKS for links" |
| Jewellery | Same neckline, 10 necklaces; same hand, 10 rings | "Comment PRICE" |
| Food / café | Same cup, 10 drinks; 10 menu items spinning | "Comment MENU" |
| Sports / gym | Same athlete, 10 drills / exercises | "Comment PLAN" |
| Gaming | Same character, 10 skins / 10 settings | "Comment SETTINGS" |
| Informational / AI tools | Same prompt, 10 outputs | "Comment PROMPTS" |
| Travel | 10 spots in one city, same framing | "Comment ITINERARY" |
| Site visit / real estate | Same room, 10 interior styles; 10 units | "Comment BROCHURE" |

## 8. Conversion layer
- This is a **comment-to-DM lead magnet**: keyword comment → ManyChat auto-DM with the asset (prompts, links, price list, menu, brochure) + email/WhatsApp capture → offer.
- Hidden list names + fast pacing drive **rewatches and saves**; the grid outro loops into the start.
- Put the keyword in the caption's first line and pin a comment repeating it.
- KPIs: keyword comments per 1k reach, DM→lead opt-in rate, CPL, replay rate / avg watch % (>80% on a 15s reel), saves per 1k reach.

## 9. Editor checklist
1. Pick ONE subject/composition; produce 10 variants (shoot, generate or source).
2. Each variant = 1.2–1.4s looping clip, same framing.
3. Black starfield bg + radial glow; title block top; list left; card right.
4. Reveal list names one by one; active = coral number + white bold.
5. Wipe card content right→left on each beat.
6. Outro: 6-card drifting grid + "Comment 'KEYWORD' for …".
7. Total 14–18s; ensure the last frame loops into the first.
