# Animation Style Pack 2 — Consistent-Character AI Prompts
(Picked from a "10 Animation styles" reel: one filmmaker character walking side-on through a film set, restyled 10 ways. AI-generated illustration quality — use these when you need full detail; use `animated-scene-engine` for code-built flat versions.)

## 1. Character bible (paste into EVERY prompt — keeps him identical across styles)
`a cheerful middle-aged filmmaker with a short brown beard, round red glasses, navy blue beret, mustard-yellow scarf, white shirt, dark navy trousers, brown shoes, holding a black-and-white film clapperboard`
Swap for a brand mascot / founder look-alike / customer persona, keeping the same 6–8 fixed traits.

## 2. Shot block (same in every prompt)
`full-body side view walking left to right, mid-stride, centred, film-set props around him (spotlight on tripod, director's chair, vintage film camera on tripod), vertical 9:16, clean composition, no text`

## 4. Animate it (image-to-video)
`side-tracking camera follows him at walking pace, natural walk cycle, background scrolls right to left with parallax, props pass by, 4–5 s, seamless loop, no morphing`

## 5. Workflow
1. Generate the character in one style → pick the best → use it as the reference image for the other 9 (character/style reference), so his features stay locked.
2. Animate each still with the animate block (Higgsfield / Kling / Runway / Veo).
3. Cut each to 1.3–2s, drop into `starfield-countdown` (list: Claymation, Anime, Pixel Art, Flat Vector, Comic Book, 3D Animation, Cartoon, Retro Low-Poly, Storybook, Toy Bricks), CTA "Comment ANIMATION for the prompts".
