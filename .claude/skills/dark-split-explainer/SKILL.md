# Dark Split Explainer — Talking-Head Playbook

Reverse-engineered from a 37s, 9:16 (720x1280, 25fps) talking-head explainer
(problem → agitation → new solution → proof → free masterclass CTA).
Feel: premium, cinematic tech-explainer. The speaker talks; the top panel *shows* what he says.

---

## 1. Layout system (two modes)

**Mode A — Split (≈75% of runtime)**
- **Top ~45% (0–575px @1280h):** near-black panel (#0B0A10 → subtle purple radial glow #2A1B4A at centre) holding the motion graphic + title.
- **Bottom ~55%:** speaker, framed chest-up, face in upper part of the panel.
- **Seam caption:** sits exactly on the boundary between panels — 1–3 words, white bold sans, soft shadow, no box.
- No divider line; the black panel simply ends on the video.

**Mode B — Full-screen speaker (≈25%)**
- Used for the **punchline / pivot / CTA lines**: "And that is the biggest problem", "So the marketers who are gonna win now…", "So we decided to host…", "Join our free live masterclass by clicking the link below".
- Caption moves to mid-chest in a **dark translucent rounded box** (rgba(30,30,30,.65), 8px radius, white bold sans).
- Usually a slightly wider/different framing (second camera angle or punch-out) = visual reset.

Switch modes on a sentence boundary with a hard cut. Rhythm: split → split → **FULL** → split … → **FULL (CTA)**.

## 2. Top-panel title formula
- `Plain word(s)` in white bold sans (**Inter Display Bold / SF Pro Bold**) + **keyword in italic serif** (**Instrument Serif Italic / Playfair Display Italic**): "Making a *Creative*", "Rush *vs* Wait", "New *AI* Tools", "Idea to Video, *Same Day*", "Free *Masterclass*", "A Brand New *Workflow*", "High Quality, *No Waiting*".
- Keyword starts white/grey and **tints to lavender #A98BFF** once the solution enters (colour = story shift from problem to solution).
- Animation: words type/fade in one by one with a slight blur-to-sharp (blur 8→0, 6–8 frames), in sync with speech.
- Position: top-centre, ~5% from top, ~30–34px @720w.

## 3. Motion-graphic vocabulary (top panel)
All in dark UI cards: #17161C fill, 1px #2A2830 border, 12–16px radius, white text, small grey labels in caps.

| Element | What it shows | Animation |
|---|---|---|
| **B-roll card + progress bar** | Footage in a rounded card, "RENDERING" chip, DAY 1–DAY 3 timeline bar | Bar fills left→right; turns **red #E8445A** when problem hits |
| **Notification toast** | iOS-style card: avatar, "Client — Need the final video today." | Slides down from top over the card, 8f ease-out |
| **Stamp label** | "2–3 DAYS" (black on white), "NOW." (white on red), "SAME DAY"/"HOURS" (white on purple #6D4AFF) | Slams in: scale 140→100% with 2–3° rotation, 4–6f, tiny shake |
| **Strikethrough swap** | "~~2–3 days~~ → HOURS" | Line draws through old value, arrow slides, new stamp slams |
| **Degrade effect** | Image card pixelates (mosaic) as "QUALITY" gets struck out in red | Pixel size ramps up over 1s |
| **Line chart** | Growth curve DAY 3→9 that crashes red at the end, label "CAMPAIGN" struck out | Line draws on, then drops red |
| **Icon row → hero icon** | 3 tool icons in rounded tiles; one becomes a big glowing logo with radiating lines + name in serif | Tiles pop in staggered; hero scales up, glow pulses |
| **Prompt box** | Chat input card typing a prompt; coloured field labels (Story:, Feel:, Likeness: in lavender) | Typewriter text + **hand-drawn curved arrows** to side labels ("Story", "Feel", "Likeness") |
| **Editor timeline** | Mini video-editor window: preview thumb + purple clip blocks on tracks, playhead | Clips drop onto track one by one, playhead sweeps, chip "Editing your video…" → "Done" |
| **Idea → Video** | Line-icon of product (draws on) + italic caption → arrow → video thumbnail, labels "IDEA" / "VIDEO" | Icon stroke draw-on, arrow draws, video card slides in |
| **Offer card** | Webinar thumbnail with "LIVE · FREE" chips + "Idea → Video *in the same day*" | Fades up, subtitle line writes in |
| **Numbered step list** | 4 rows (Prompt / Story / Edit / Export) with icons + sub-labels; "Built on X" chip | Rows dim; each number circle lights **purple** in turn as it's mentioned |
| **Result cards** | Product shot + "Today" progress card 0%→97%→READY, check badges, labels "QUALITY" "NO WAITING" | Progress counts up, purple check badges pop |

Rule: **one idea per graphic, one graphic per sentence (3–6s)**, built up piece by piece while the line is spoken — never fully revealed at once.

## 4. Speaker footage & grade
- Low-key, cinematic: dark room, teal/green-grey wall with **venetian-blind shadow pattern**, warm practical lamp(s) for orange highlights, plant in background, shallow depth of field.
- Grade: teal shadows / warm highlights, crushed-but-soft blacks to match the black top panel, neutral skin.
- Speaker in a plain light tee (pops against dark bg). Hands active in frame.
- Jump cuts on every pause; occasional 105–115% punch-in for emphasis.

## 5. Captions
- **Seam caption (split mode):** white **Inter/SF Pro Bold ~26px@720w**, lowercase/sentence case, 1–3 words, swap per phrase, no keyword colour.
- **Full-screen caption:** same font inside dark translucent box, mid-chest.
- No emojis, no stickers — clean, premium.

## 6. Audio
- Voice-led; subtle low ambient/tech bed. SFX: soft whoosh on panel changes, "thunk" on stamp slams, notification ding on the toast, UI clicks/typing on prompt box.

## 7. Story template (30–45s)

| Beat | Mode | Top-panel graphic |
|---|---|---|
| Hook: the slow/old way ("Making a creative takes 2–3 days") | Split | B-roll card + DAY progress bar + "2–3 DAYS" stamp |
| Agitate ("but your client wants it NOW") | Split | Notification toast + "NOW." red stamp, bar turns red |
| Name it ("And that is the biggest problem…") | **Full** | — |
| Stakes ("Rushing kills quality, waiting kills the campaign") | Split | "Rush *vs* Wait": pixelated card vs crashing chart |
| Solution ("New tools like X") | Split | Icon row → hero logo |
| How it works (2–3 lines) | Split | Prompt box with arrows → editor timeline → "~~2–3 days~~ → HOURS" |
| Payoff / who wins | **Full** | — |
| Reframe ("Idea to video, same day") | Split | Idea → Video graphic + "SAME DAY" stamp |
| Offer intro ("So we decided to host…") | **Full** | — |
| Offer details | Split | Offer card → numbered step list → result cards |
| **CTA** ("Join our free live masterclass — link below") | **Full** | — |

## 8. Adapting by niche
- **Agency / B2B / SaaS:** as above; graphics = dashboards, funnels, ROAS charts, Slack/email toasts.
- **Real estate / site visit:** top panel = floor-plan card, price stamp, location pin map, amenities step list; bottom = agent walking/talking.
- **Coach / finance / health:** before-vs-after chart, checklist step list, "30 DAYS" stamp.
- **D2C / food / fashion founder:** product line-icon draw-on → product photo card, price strikethrough → offer stamp.
- Swap the purple accent for the brand colour; keep the dark panel + one alarm colour (red) for the problem.

## 9. Conversion layer
- The structure *is* a direct-response script: problem → agitation → stakes → solution → proof → offer → CTA.
- CTA always in **full-screen mode**, spoken + boxed caption, plus a stamp on the last split graphic (FREE / LIVE / LIMITED SEATS — only if true).
- Pair with "comment KEYWORD" or link-in-bio/landing page; pin the registration link.
- KPIs: 3s hold >60% (hook graphic), avg watch time >50%, link CTR >1%, cost per registration/lead (CPL), registration→attendance rate, and downstream show-up→sale.

## 10. Editor checklist
1. Cut silences; mark which sentences are punchlines → full-screen; the rest → split.
2. Build a black 720x575 (or 1080x860) top panel with purple radial glow.
3. One graphic per sentence, revealed piece by piece in sync with speech.
4. Title per graphic: sans + italic-serif keyword; keyword goes lavender after the solution appears.
5. Stamps for numbers/time words (2–3 DAYS, NOW., HOURS, SAME DAY, FREE).
6. Seam captions in split mode; boxed captions in full mode.
7. Teal/warm low-key grade on speaker; match blacks to panel.
8. SFX on every stamp, toast and typing.
9. End on full-screen CTA.
