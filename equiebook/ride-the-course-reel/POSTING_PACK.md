# EquiEbook: "Ride the Course" interactive POV reel

**Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · ~33 s · Instagram Reels (primary), YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels (cross-post)
**Mechanic:** The viewer puts their thumbs on the rider's gloves and follows the on-screen prompts (JUMP!, LEFT, RIGHT, AGAIN!, LAST ONE!) through a 7-fence show-jumping course. Each prompt pulses the matching thumb ring, so it feels like the viewer is the one riding.
**Business goal:** App installs (iOS + Android). Secondary: shares and saves that feed reach.

Brand compliance: deep brown, antique gold and cream palette only (no neon). Lora serif headlines, Poppins for UI. No FEI endorsement claim. "India's equestrian app", never "only".
